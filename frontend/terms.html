Terms of use

By using Saywell you agree to the points below.

What Saywell is

A communication coach that suggests ways to say things. Suggestions are options, not advice about who is right, and not a replacement for professional help.

Your responsibility

  • You decide what you send and to whom. Read every suggestion before you use it.
  • Do not use Saywell to harass, threaten or manipulate anyone. Saywell will decline to shape messages for that purpose.
  • Do not paste other people's private information you have no right to share.

Safety

Saywell will not rewrite messages that suggest immediate danger or self-harm. If you or someone else might be in danger, contact local emergency services.

Availability

The service is provided as it is. AI output can be wrong or awkward. It may be unavailable at times.
