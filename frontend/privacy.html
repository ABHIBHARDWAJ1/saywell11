Your words are personal.

This page explains, in plain words, what Saywell does with what you write.

What is sent where

  • When you press Analyze, Reply, Repair or Practice, your text is sent to the Saywell server, which forwards it to an AI provider (Groq) to write the response. The pattern detector runs on the Saywell server itself.
  • The try-a-sample box on the home page works the same way, without an account, and saves nothing.
  • As you type on Analyze, the text is sent to the Saywell server to show live patterns.
  • Screenshots are read on your own device (OCR). The image is not uploaded. Only the text you confirm is analyzed.

What Saywell saves

  • Your Google name, email and photo, through Google sign-in, to identify your account.
  • Your analyses (History), phrases you save (Library), finished practice sessions, lesson progress and settings. These are stored in your account and follow you across devices.
  • Nothing is saved to History when you switch on “Don't save to history” for a message, or turn off “Save my analyses to history” in Settings. That controls what Saywell keeps. It does not mean the text is never transmitted.

Your choices

  • Export your saved data, delete all history, or delete your account, from Settings.
  • The Chrome extension only checks text when you type in its popup, right-click a selection, or press its shortcut. It does not read pages in the background.

Not a crisis service

Saywell is a communication coach. It is not therapy, medical advice or emergency help. If someone may be in danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis line.
