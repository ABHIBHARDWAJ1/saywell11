THE METHOD

From reaction to connection.

Saywell is built on Nonviolent Communication. Four small steps, one sentence at a time.

1 Observe

What happened, without the verdict

“You never listen” becomes “When I was speaking, the conversation changed direction.”

2 Feel

What you may be feeling

Offered as options, never as facts about you. Frustrated, hurt, lonely, unsure.

3 Need

What matters underneath

Being heard, respect, reliability, clarity, rest.

4 Request

One thing they can say yes to

“Would you let me finish before responding?” A request, not a demand.

Clearer does not mean softer.

Boundary, not demand

A demand is about what they must do. A boundary is about what you will do: “If it turns into insults, I will step away and we can talk later.”

Your anger can stay

Saywell removes the attack and the verdict, not your frustration or your limit.

Your voice stays yours

Hinglish stays Hinglish. Short stays short.
